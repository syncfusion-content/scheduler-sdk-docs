# Quick Info Template in TypeScript Scheduler

The Scheduler control displays a pop-up window (Quick Info) when users click cells or appointments. By default, Quick Info shows standard information. You can customize its appearance and content using templates to better suit your application requirements.

This guide demonstrates how to create custom templates for Quick Info pop-ups that appear when clicking empty time cells and appointments in the Scheduler control.
